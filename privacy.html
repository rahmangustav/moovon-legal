Privacy Policy

Moovon Publisher — last updated 22 September 2026

1. Summary

Moovon Publisher is an internal tool that lets one business, Moovon, publish its own videos to its own TikTok account. It does not collect data from the public or from other TikTok users.

2. What we access

  • Account identifier (open_id) and basic profile of the Moovon TikTok account, to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok when the owner authorizes the Tool.
  • Video files and captions that the owner chooses to publish.

We do not read comments, direct messages, follower lists, or any information about other TikTok users.

3. How data is stored

Tokens and video files are stored on a private computer controlled by Moovon, protected by file permissions and not exposed to the internet. Nothing is stored in a shared or third-party database.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, in order to publish the owner’s video.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the connection is active. The owner can revoke access in TikTok’s app settings at any time; we then delete the stored tokens. To request deletion of any stored data, email us at the address below.

6. Children

The Tool is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone other than the business owner.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
